# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 July 2026_

CreaVerse ("we", "us", "our") is an education platform operated for the benefit of students, teachers, and administrators. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use, share, and protect it.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information** you provide at sign-up: full name, email address, gender (optional), current school/college/academy (optional), and role (student / teacher / admin).
- **Classroom activity**: classes you create or join, assignments you publish or submit, quiz answers, chat messages inside a class, and files you upload.
- **AI Mentor conversations**: the messages you send to and receive from our AI Mentor, stored per user so history can be restored.
- **Journal entries**: private daily reflections, visible only to you.
- **Onboarding survey responses**: your one-time answers about grade, discovery source, purpose, and learning challenges.
- **Feedback**: rating and comments you voluntarily submit.
- **Technical data**: standard server logs, session tokens, and error reports.

## 2. How we use your information

- To provide the core service: authenticate you, show your classes, deliver AI Mentor replies, sync your journal, and let teachers grade your work.
- To operate our platform (security, abuse prevention, debugging).
- To improve CreaVerse in aggregate (e.g. onboarding analytics show *counts* to admins, not individual identities where not required).

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 3. Sharing

- **Teachers** can see the roster, submissions, and quiz answers of students enrolled in their own classes.
- **Admins** can see aggregated onboarding analytics and can manage user roles/suspensions.
- **Service providers** we rely on (hosting, database, auth, AI inference gateway) process data on our behalf under standard contractual protections.
- We disclose information when required by applicable law.

## 4. Data retention

We keep account data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing **support@creaverse.site**. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate operational needs.

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard security: encrypted transport (HTTPS), row-level-security database policies scoped to authenticated users, and least-privilege service credentials. No system is perfectly secure; you use the service at your own risk.

## 6. Children

CreaVerse is intended for use by students, including minors, in an educational setting. Where required, we rely on parents, guardians, teachers, or schools to supervise account creation and provide any necessary consent.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing **support@creaverse.site**.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of CreaVerse after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## 9. Contact

Questions or requests: **support@creaverse.site**
